================================================================================
LOAD DISPATCHER BEHAVIOR (VA 0x5B3D60) - absent chunks & count field
================================================================================
- The dispatcher iterates the REGISTERED savables vector ([this+4] begin/end),
  NOT the file's record list, and NOT the count field.
- Pairing is POSITIONAL (lockstep): savable[i] is compared against record[i]
  (record chain starts at tree+0x1C0+0xC = 0x1CC, advances raw
  slot += size + 0xC). On id mismatch the chunk is SKIPPED (defaults kept,
  no reject) and BOTH iterators advance.
- Consequences for converted saves:
    * MISSING TRAILING chunks (e.g. dropping PCControllerSettings,
      CustomRaceDayMemcard, UnlockSystem) are tolerated - the extra savables
      read buffer filler as records, mismatch, and are skipped.
    * A missing MIDDLE chunk (or reordering) DESYNCS every later pairing -
      not a rejection, but all subsequent chunks silently fail to load.
      Keep the registered order and pad missing middle chunks with a dummy
      record (e.g. flags=1, size 0) if later chunks must survive.
- tree+0x10 count field: written from the savable-vector length at save time
  (0x5AAD03); NOT read and NOT validated anywhere on the load path. Purely
  informational (it does equal the number of top-level records: 15/9 here).
